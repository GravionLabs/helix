Helix Shell wrappers

Components of @gravionlabs/helix-shell that remove boilerplate of admin pages.

Page header

Breadcrumb, title, subtitle and the actions of a page; the actions wrap under the title on narrow screens.

Stat card

Label, value, icon and trend; the trend direction is an arrow plus the words "up", "down" or "flat" for screen readers.

Empty state

Page level (medium) and inside the empty row of a data table (small, through hxTableEmpty).
